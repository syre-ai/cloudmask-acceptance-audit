Cloud-mask acceptance audit

OmniCloudMask v4 on Sentinel-2 · Independent model assurance report · 2026-10-27
Prepared by Shane Cairns · All figures computed from public data by the accompanying repository
Question. Before automating on OmniCloudMask, how much cloud and shadow reaches the pixels we accept, how much clear ground do we lose, where does it fail, and which setting should we run?

Summary

  • Usable, with a setting change. OmniCloudMask had the highest balanced accuracy of the masks tested at their default settings (0.942); it leaks 6.1% of accepted pixels by default and 2.0% at the recommended threshold.
  • Do not filter scenes with the SCL band. Of the patches it keeps under a 10% cloud rule, 43.4% are cloudier than that.
  • Expect more leak over water (11.0%) and missed cloud in mostly clear scenes (16.6% of it).
  • Mask native-grid, unclipped data where possible. In simulation, 30 m data put on a 10 m grid discards 24.6% of clear ground (native grid: 6.6%); reflectance clipped at 0.3 raises leak by +13.3 pp.

Test setup

Data. CloudSEN12+ expert-labeled test split: 960 Sentinel-2 scenes (5.1 km, 10 m) at 192 sites worldwide, 2018-2020, at L1C and L2A. The validation split was used only to choose operating points.

Independence from training data. The model's published training set includes CloudSEN12 material from every split, so its 100,528 training records were checked directly against the test scenes: no shared scene ids (including 2,754 test/validation-tagged training records), no shared acquisitions; 10 scenes that may overlap a custom training scene were removed. Removing every scene on a Kappaset training tile gives leak 6.2% (headline 6.1%). This screen has limits; it does not prove that no overlap remains.

Comparators. Sentinel-2 QA60 and Sen2Cor SCL (shipped with the products), s2cloudless (upstream defaults), Cloud Score+ (cs ≥ 0.60), UnetMobV2 and SEnSeI v2.

Metrics. Leak: share of accepted pixels that are cloud or shadow. Discard: share of clear pixels rejected. Miss: share of cloud/shadow accepted. Scene filter: keep a 5.1 km patch if estimated cloud+shadow cover ≤ 10%. All methods are scored on the same pixels; abstentions count as rejected. 95% confidence intervals by bootstrap over sites; comparisons are paired.

Severity. High: affects a default decision path, large error, measured on real data. Medium: material error in a common condition on real data, a trade-off the client must set, or a large simulated effect on a common processing path. Low: limited evidence or moderate simulated effects. Info: checked, small or no measured effect.

Findings, ranked by severity

#SeverityFinding and evidenceRecommendation
F1HighScene filters built on the Sen2Cor SCL band keep cloudy scenes
On the 5.1 km test patches, keeping a patch when its SCL cloud+shadow cover is at most 10% keeps patches of which 43.4% are above 10% by the expert labels; SCL under-reports cover by -13 pp on average. The same rule using OmniCloudMask cover: 5.9% above, and 4.0% of good patches rejected (SCL: 16.6%).		Gate on OmniCloudMask-derived cover instead of the SCL band. Measured: cloud+shadow inside kept patches after masking falls from 3.4% to 1.6% [0.7, 2.6]. Full-granule filtering was not tested.
F2MediumAt its default setting, about 6% of accepted pixels are cloud or shadow
Leak 6.1% [5.3, 7.0] of accepted pixels (thin cloud 2.9%, shadow 1.9%, thick 1.3%); discard 4.6% [4.0, 5.3] of clear pixels.		For leak-sensitive uses (time series, change detection): accept if P(clear) ≥ 0.8529 (chosen on the validation split for a 2% leak target). Test: leak 2.0% [1.4, 2.6] at 13.6% discard. Buffering the mask is the weaker lever: none of the tested buffers (0 to 8 px) met the target; 8 px leaks 3.6% at 13.1% discard.
F3MediumCoarse data resampled onto a finer grid is over-masked (simulated)
Simulated 30 m data put on a 10 m grid with bilinear interpolation: discard 24.6% (+20.0 pp); with nearest-neighbour instead: 16.3% (+11.7 pp); the same 30 m data masked on its own grid: 6.6% (+2.0 pp). Native-grid runs at 20/30/50 m: +1.2 pp / +2.0 pp / +3.6 pp.		Where the pipeline allows, run the mask on the sensor's native grid and resample the mask. If imagery must be resampled to a finer grid first, validate the mask on that product; real resamplers and sensor optics differ from this simulation.
F4MediumClipping bright reflectance lets cloud through (simulated)
Clipping at 0.3 reflectance (close to the Sentinel-2 true-colour stretch) raises leak by +13.3 pp with 16-bit values and +13.3 pp when also quantized to 8 bits; clipping at 1.0: +0.0 pp. JPEG on top of 8-bit (clip 0.6, +2.4 pp) adds little at quality 95 (+3.1 pp in total) and more at quality 50 (+6.3 pp).		Feed unclipped reflectance; avoid running the mask on stretched visual products.
F5MediumHigher leak over water (11.0% vs 6.1% overall)
Leak over water 11.0% [6.3, 15.9] across 98 sites (thick cloud 4.2% vs 0.9% over grassland); the two worst sites hold 34% of it.		For coastal, inland-water and flood work, spot-check a local sample; the stricter threshold above was not validated separately for water.
F6MediumSmall amounts of cloud are missed in mostly clear scenes
In scenes with 1-30% cloud, 16.6% [13.7, 19.8] of the cloud and shadow present is accepted as clear (overcast scenes: 3.3%). These are the scenes a cover gate keeps.		The threshold recommendation reduces leak overall; it was not tuned for this regime separately.
F7MediumUsable ground is discarded in heavily clouded scenes
In scenes with 70-99% cloud, 19.7% [17.2, 22.4] of the clear pixels are rejected.		If gap-filling from cloudy scenes matters, validate a lower threshold for that regime (not tested here).
F8LowSnow and ice: highest observed leak, weakest evidence
Leak 13.0% [6.4, 18.4] over only 14 sites, 67% of it from two sites, mostly thin cloud over snow, where expert labels are least certain (not adjudicated here).		Validate locally before relying on the mask in snow-covered regions.
F9LowAdded sensor noise increases leak (simulated)
Gaussian noise with a standard deviation of 0.005 reflectance raises leak by +2.6 pp [1.9, 3.6]; 0.01 by +6.7 pp.		Re-validate before using the mask on noisier imagery than Sentinel-2.
F10InfoSmall measured effects within the tested range
L1C vs L2A input: leak +0.2 pp [0.0, 0.5]. One tested per-band gain/offset change: 99.99% pixel agreement on a fifth of the test scenes (the model normalizes each band per image). NIR shifted 4 px diagonally: discard +0.9 pp [0.6, 1.2], leak +0.1 pp [-0.2, 0.5].		None required within these ranges; larger shifts or other calibration errors were not tested.

Recommendations

  1. Gate scenes on OmniCloudMask cover instead of the Sen2Cor SCL band.
  2. For leak-sensitive uses, accept pixels only if P(clear) ≥ 0.8529.
  3. Prefer masking on the native grid; validate before masking resampled imagery.
  4. Feed unclipped reflectance, not stretched visual products.
  5. Spot-check a local sample before relying on the mask over water.
  6. If you gap-fill from cloudy scenes, validate a lower threshold for them.

Limitations

  • Expert labels are least certain for thin cloud over snow and at cloud edges; some measured leak there may be label disagreement (not adjudicated).
  • The test split is cloudier than a typical archive; per-regime results allow reweighting to a client's own cloud climatology.
  • Sentinel-2 only; the model's sensor-agnostic claim was not tested. Degradations are simple controlled simulations, not models of a particular sensor or pipeline.
  • Scene-filter results are for 5.1 km patches, not full granules. Condition-specific recommendations were not validated separately.
